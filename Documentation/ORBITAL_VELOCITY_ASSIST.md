# Orbital velocity and orbit hold

While piloting, **press N to toggle orbit hold on or off**. It captures your current planet-relative radius and orbital plane, then applies bounded velocity corrections to return to that radius, reduce radial drift and maintain the plane. It continues while you walk around the ship. Press N again to return to unpowered coasting. Translational thrust, braking, warp, docking/landing transitions or enabling hover assist with V cancel orbit hold. Steering and L attitude hold remain available.

Circular tangential speed is `sqrt(mu / r)` at the current radius. Altitude and plane feedback correct disturbances instead of repeatedly adopting a drifting radius. With no meaningful tangential motion, the projected hull heading supplies a plane; retrograde motion is preserved. **Left Shift** boosts correction from 40 to 160 m/s² while piloting. Correction stays capped at the remaining velocity error. Hover assist stays off when orbit hold is disabled; V enables it again.

Take off and climb clear of the atmosphere and terrain envelope first. The HUD shows ON/OFF, held altitude, target velocity and any clearance requirement. The controller cannot avoid arbitrary obstructions or guarantee safety when it lacks sufficient acceleration. It is powered stationkeeping, not a claim that the same orbit remains stable without corrections in a multi-body system.

The numerical galaxy forecast captures the enabled controller, including held radius, plane and available acceleration. Its label distinguishes **POWERED ORBIT HOLD** from **PREDICTED COAST**. The 160 samples and existing per-frame work budget remain unchanged. A bound trajectory can extend the configured horizon by up to four times to cover a full orbit; windows that still cannot cover one period are labelled PARTIAL. No line is forced closed. Current-planet impact checks sample actual terrain and ocean height within the broad terrain envelope. Genuine future intersections still stop the forecast.

Unpowered central-gravity flight now uses double-precision velocity Verlet integration, matching the approach already used by galaxy gravity. A complete 14 km-radius circular-orbit fixture reduced radial range from 13.235555 m with the previous Euler step to 0.006256 m; peak radial speed was 0.00005915 m/s at 50 ms steps. This addresses numerical oscillation; real eccentricity and external gravity can still produce radial motion.

The diagnostics under **Synthetic Front → Diagnostics → Validate orbital velocity and toggle hold** pass 24 checks using the production integrator, radius recovery from disturbances, toggling off, manual override, full-period coast accuracy and the actual galaxy predictor. In the moving-body test, orbit hold varied radius by 0.006389 m over a complete orbit, with peak radial speed 0.00122881 m/s. The 140 km-radius powered forecast completed its period with a 72.656 m endpoint difference, without forcing the line closed. These are fixture measurements, not a guarantee for every altitude or multi-body encounter.

Results are in `TestResults/Planetary/OrbitVelocity/validation.json`. Frozen pre-change sources and the cancelled standalone build report are under `TestResults/Planetary/OrbitHold`.
