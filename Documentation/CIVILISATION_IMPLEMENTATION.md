Ordinary play asynchronously reads 16-byte mission summaries at up to 10 Hz; validation alone can synchronously read those events. Search scratch allocates to current power-of-two road/fleet capacity; configured worst case 32768 nodes x128 slots =64 MiB, excluding small graph/job/pose buffers. No distant cargo dispatch. Offline catch-up uses aggregate mission ETAs instead of live road searches.

18 October 2026 — households and civilian routines (accepted after optimization): family homes now hold at most six people, with one household per building and six serviced starter homes per faction. Owner-occupied, private rental and council homes are separate placeable catalogue entries. Home upgrades no longer turn these into large invisible apartment blocks. Initial population remains 24 (20 adults and four children), with names, stable IDs, ages, parent/partner relationships, inherited background, education, reputation, savings, employment and household membership. Existing population migration becomes explicit family reunification; children age on a configurable calendar, education requires a connected staffed school, and adult children can establish households in real vacant homes. Births require a supplied family with room. This is a strategic society model; marriage formation, mortality, property sale/mortgage auctions and a full political simulation are not implemented.

Worker and Engineer assignments contribute to ordinary paid construction/repairs, and the workforce counts working-age adults. Gatherers staff farms/extraction and new timber/salvage camps. Gathering uses finite, saved regional reserves, so rebuilding a camp does not replenish the same region. Goods use the existing local inventories, cargo transport and warehouse accounting. Unemployed adults are Survivors. These are civilian jobs, not additional army archetypes. Existing infantry/engineer art is reused for instanced citizens.

SettlementCitizens owns separate GPU pedestrian records and shared reverse road BFS fields per building destination. Strategic people have no CPU transform or army mirror and no per-person GameObjects. Routes follow the same faction road graph as cargo; retargeting occurs at road nodes without resetting a live GPU pose. People commute to work/school, a household-designated adult shops in the afternoon, and everyone is sent home at night. People disappear inside their destination. Distant pedestrian motion sleeps outside the draw-interest range, and inactive planets do not dispatch this subsystem. Rendering is instanced and distance/hemisphere culled. Configured initial cap is 16,384 GPU civilian slots per active planet; road search storage is shared by destinations, never allocated per pedestrian. Occupation/production still use coarse economic staffing rather than GPU arrival readbacks; the daily financial and retail model is also aggregate, not individual bags of physical groceries.

The local credit ledger tracks finite enterprise capital, wage payments, wage and sales taxes, rent, purchases, procurement, welfare, school funding and construction contracts. Starting treasury and immigrant savings are explicit external endowments; transfers within an existing community conserve credits. Newborns start with zero savings. Businesses cannot pay wages beyond available cash. Councils choose weekly priorities from community needs, and the player can set a funding mandate. AI civic construction consumes the normal material wallet and road budgets; private projects pay a local contract. Fixed-price procurement is a simplified connected-business circulation model, not a full supply-chain price discovery or insolvency market. Resident biographies, ownership and these ledgers are included in the existing strategic save format; the prior tactical-save limitation remains.

Wheat and all matching crop definitions first try abutting parcels with shared orientation/frontage, then fall back to bounded district placement. Fences, walls, gates and existing armed turrets remain paid placeable defences. Gate footprints allow only a one-cell road passage. Closing a gate disconnects road navigation and restores its collision/nav obstacle; opening it restores road links and leaves physical pillar colliders. AI can add a compact gated HQ strongpoint alongside its existing turret programme; this is not an automatically enclosing city perimeter. Gates are manually controlled in building inspection, and the AI's starter strongpoint gate is left open for traffic. Automated friendly gate sensing is not implemented.

The original RTS HUD adds RESIDENTS with paged biographies, household and employment data, council finances/funding choices and business accounts. Home inspection shows family members and ownership, including read-only AI Test inspection. All prior construction categories and resource screens remain.

Additional scope notes: the daily routine currently uses the configurable civic clock (10-minute days and a 365-day year by default), rather than a longitude-based solar clock synchronized with rotating planets/binary illumination. Civilian records are not yet combat targets. Corpses, pregnancy, marriage formation, household relocation between planets and full property markets remain future extensions. These do not alter the existing GPU army/combat pipeline. Timber and scrap can now be processed into construction materials through alternate fabricator recipes; autonomous fabricators may select those when their normal inputs are unavailable and salvage/timber exists.

Gate follow-up: closed-gate cells are cached once per road revision, rather than scanning every building for every road edge.
