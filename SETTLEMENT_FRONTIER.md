# Settlement alliances, pipelines and the frontier

Start a new planetary session after Unity recompiles. Starting locations and camps
are generated at session creation; planet size and weather settings are unchanged.

## Starting settlements

`PlanetDevelopment` exposes **Allied Starting Settlements**, **Country Count**
(four by default), **Settlement Minimum Separation** and **Settlement Maximum
Separation** (15 km / 45 km). The starting towns have independent economies,
inventories and autonomous construction planners. Your command centre is the
capital; three or more members constitute a country. Command-centre inspection
and map markers identify the capital and allied towns.

Dry coastal plains are preferred. Placement preserves the spacing constraints
instead of placing another town in water. Inter-town roads extend in paid,
bounded sections as construction reserves allow. Destroying an allied town's HQ
does not award a victory against that ally.

## Oil and gas

Build **Oil Storage Depot** or **Gas Storage Depot** from the construction drawer.
Oil pipes are slate coloured; methane pipes are yellow. Complete, powered
facilities automatically receive paid pipe connections along connected road
corridors. A depot also has a **Connect Oil/Gas Pipeline** inspection action.
Pipelines carry actual finite inventory, stop at capacity and stop without fuel.
Depot pairs balance stock; extractors/docks supply storage and storage supplies
refineries. The default pumping fuel cost is 10% of the equivalent loaded
lorry's round-trip cost, excluding pipe construction.

Depots order a dedicated tanker through the light depot when roads, fleet space
and construction reserves permit. Tankers collect directly from oil/gas
extractors and docks, and deliver refinery inputs. Incoming sea freight unloads
oil/gas into the receiving dock inventory before its final road leg; full docks
leave unaccepted cargo aboard. Other cargo retains the existing freight flow.

## Raiders and infection

Civilisation settings expose raider enable/count, detailed range, strategic
tick, ambush range and raid interval. Three camps are generated by default.
Camps have their own loot stores and GPU combat targets. Nearby guards use
ordinary GPU army records, with no per-unit GameObjects or CPU pose mirror.
The **Crime** view shows camps, shipment incidents and outbreak estimates; the
incident map marks camps and ambushes. Police fight raiders through the existing
GPU civilian combat system, and successful raids request additional patrols.

Distant raids use bounded mission-corridor decisions. Nearby raids require a
GPU truck-position event near an active camp. Theft transfers real loaded cargo
into the camp's inventory, including registered gold/currency cargo when present.
No gold or currency shipments are manufactured automatically.

Ambient zombies and automatic waves are disabled in normal planetary sessions.
**Allow Legacy Zombie Spawning** preserves opt-in legacy scenarios; explicit
standalone validation fixtures retain their original setup.

Research **Bio-warfare research**, then **Infection payloads** at an advanced
laboratory. An operational silo gains **Infection Bomb** targeting. Infantry and
residents within the blast can convert, and zombie contact spreads infection.
Zombies wander at about 1.4 m/s and pursue at 3.5 m/s in the new planetary mode.

Distant outbreaks use population density, infected population and periodically
counted GPU zombies. Named conversion bookkeeping is bounded; aggregate civilian
conversions materialise as GPU actors when the player returns. Returning actors
are deducted from the aggregate estimate. Off-world economies use their existing
round-robin background tick, including pipe flows and outbreak estimates.

## Processing and verification

Distant strategic army actors stagger detailed sensing, combat and movement
over 32 cohorts, integrating elapsed movement time. Nearby actors use detailed
simulation. Civilian road motion uses a coarser distant cadence. Pipeline tube
geometry is batched, and shared corridor geometry is deduplicated.

Offline checks are in `TestResults/Frontier`: managed inventory/alliance and
actual catalogue validation, runtime C# compilation, normal/enabled compute
shader variants, and pipeline/camp material stages. These do not validate a
Unity playthrough or certify the 60 FPS/million-unit acceptance target. Equivalent
standalone median/p95 frame time, simulation rate, memory and fog measurements
remain required before performance acceptance.
